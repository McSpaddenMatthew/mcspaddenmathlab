What this actually is

McSpadden Math Lab is tutoring — with structure, pressure, and challenge built in. Students solve real problems, explain their thinking, play strategic games, and train mental flexibility.

This is for kids who already “do fine” in class but need more stretch, higher expectations, and a place where thinking hard is normal.
