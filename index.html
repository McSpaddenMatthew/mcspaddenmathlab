Questions Parents Ask

Is this regular tutoring?

Yes — this is tutoring, but with more structure and challenge. We review what your child is learning, clear up confusion, and then go further with problems, games, and discussions that push their thinking.

Is this for struggling students?

This lab is designed for capable students who can handle more and need to be pushed. If your child is significantly below grade level, traditional one-on-one remediation may be a better first step.

What changes will I actually notice in my child?

Parents usually see a few things quickly:

• They shift from bored to more engaged in math. • They start checking their work because they want to be accurate. • They are less shaken by hard problems. • They begin to see themselves as “good at math,” not just “getting by.”

What if we have to miss a Saturday?

No problem. Because the focus is on deep understanding and habits, your child can rejoin the next week and still benefit. I make sure they know the key idea or strategy the group built so they’re not lost.

My child is already advanced. Is this still valuable?

Yes. Advanced students often move quickly through material but haven’t had space to struggle productively or explain their thinking. Here, they’re pushed with deeper problems and encouraged to think out loud, justify their choices, and try more than one approach.

How big are the groups?

Pods are capped at 6 students. That’s small enough for every child to talk, be seen, and be pushed, and big enough for them to learn from one another.

What is the schedule and price?

We meet Saturdays at the Haymarket / Gainesville Library:

• 2nd–3rd Grade: 10:30–11:25 AM
• 4th–5th Grade: 11:30 AM–12:25 PM

Tuition is $340/month. That includes all Saturday sessions in that month.

Do you offer a guarantee?

I stand behind the work. If after the first month you don’t see any shift in how your child approaches math — their confidence, their willingness to try, their engagement — you may cancel with no further commitment.
