Who this phase is for

Designed for students currently in Grade 3 who are capable but:

✓Work slowly because facts are not automatic
✓Lose accuracy when problems have multiple steps
✓Rely on counting, guessing, or repeated re-checking
✓Feel confidence drop as math speeds up

This phase is intentionally narrow. We do not “cover everything.” We secure the foundations that determine whether 4th-grade math works.

How Grade 4 Readiness works

A fixed sequence designed to remove guesswork and build momentum.
Step 1: Math Readiness Clearance
The Guide

Student performance is monitored continuously throughout the phase. Based on what each student demonstrates in sessions, families receive a simple 10-minute home protocol aligned to current needs. The goal is targeted reinforcement—not additional work.

Step 2: Live Readiness Sessions
The Work

Cohorts meet in small groups twice per week at the Gainesville–Haymarket Library. Sessions are structured to build automaticity, number sense, and accuracy under mild pressure.

Step 3: Transfer & Application
The Hold

Each week, students apply skills through concepts, puzzles, and real problem-solving. If a skill only works in one setting, it is not yet secure.

What’s included

The Readiness Support Stack • built for visible, durable gains

1) Must-Know Math Skills Framework (Grades 1–6)

A clear, parent-facing framework of the non-negotiable skills that predict long-term math success (fluency-first: number sense, automaticity, foundational computation).

2) Weekly Parent Snapshot

A short weekly update explaining what was targeted, why it matters, and what’s next. No dashboards. No micromanaging. Just clarity.

3) Home Protocol Card

A one-page, 10-minute routine: exactly what to do, for exactly how long, and when to stop—designed to prevent regression without homework battles.

4) Mid-Phase Readiness Checkpoint

A concise midpoint update outlining what has shifted, what still needs reinforcement, and whether the current trajectory is on track—so adjustments happen early.

How to decide if this phase is right

This phase is not about getting ahead. It’s about being ready. Grade 4 Readiness is a good fit if two or more of the following are true:

✓ Homework takes too long for what should be basic work—even when understanding is there
✓ Accuracy drops when problems include multiple steps
✓ Your child still relies on counting, guessing, or repeated re-checking for facts that should be automatic
✓ Your child is capable, but hesitates because they don’t trust their answer
✓ Small errors (place value, regrouping, operation choice) show up repeatedly and compound across a page
✓ Math is starting to feel like a daily fight rather than something they can do calmly

If you’re unsure, that’s normal. The Math Readiness Clearance exists to remove guesswork. If your child does not need this phase, we will say so.
