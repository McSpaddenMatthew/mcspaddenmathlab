The Math Readiness Path

Grade 4 Readiness

A 10-week intervention designed to secure the math foundations students need before fourth-grade expectations accelerate.

This is not tutoring. It is a time-boxed readiness phase built for clarity, confidence, and durable skill development.

Apply for March Enrollment

Who This Phase Is For

This phase is designed for:

  • Students currently in Grade 3
  • Students early in Grade 4
  • Children who understand concepts but work slowly or inconsistently
  • Students whose confidence drops as math speeds up

If your child simply needs more worksheets, this is not a fit. If your child needs the right foundations secured before expectations rise, it is.

How the Path Works

Step 1

Math Readiness Clearance

A short diagnostic to identify which skills are solid, which are fragile, and what actually matters right now.

Step 2

Live Small-Group Sessions

Twice-weekly, structured sessions focused on automaticity, number sense, and accuracy under mild pressure.

Step 3

Transfer & Application

Skills are applied through games and problem-solving so they hold outside the session and show up in school.
