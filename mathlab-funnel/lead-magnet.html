7-Day Reset (simple steps)

  1. Pick one rung: subtraction (most common) or the first skill that causes slowdown.
  2. Do 5 minutes daily: quick verbal practice (no counting, no worksheets).
  3. Use one success rule: answers in ~2 seconds without counting.
  4. Only then move up: don’t stack skills or “do today’s classwork” as practice.
Why this works: math is hierarchical. When you try to fix everything at once, kids get learning interference — progress feels random and slow.
