Who it’s for

  • Kids who understand lessons but slow down on the basics.
  • Kids who hesitate on facts, regrouping, or place value and it shows up in word problems.
  • Families who want a clear plan instead of chasing worksheets.
  • Grades 2–6 who want the next grade to feel easier.

Who it’s not for

  • Families looking for night-by-night homework help.
  • Kids who need 1:1 specialized services beyond small-group instruction.
  • Families who can’t attend consistently (two sessions/week).
