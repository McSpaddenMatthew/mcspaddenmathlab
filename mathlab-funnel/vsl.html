It starts with hesitation

Before a kid says “I don’t like math,” they stall on basics.

What it looks like
  • “8 × 6 is… um…”
  • “Is 1/4 the same as 0.25?”
  • “0.5… is that half?”
  • Multi-digit subtraction feels slow + uncertain
  • 997 vs 1,002—closer to 1,000?
What happens next
  • Basics aren’t automatic → math takes too much effort
  • Effort becomes frustration
  • Frustration becomes avoidance
  • A year later: “I don’t like math.”
Grades can hide gaps. Heavier math exposes them.
Important: This is designed to prevent the “math turns on them later” moment.
